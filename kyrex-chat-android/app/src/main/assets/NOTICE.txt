Kyrex for Android

This application bundles the Kyrex Chat host, Health, Voice and Messages
adapters. The corresponding source, build scripts and dependency versions are
available at https://github.com/kp84-hub/kyrex.

The Messages adapter and its Go bridge use AGPL-3.0-or-later; COPYING-AGPL.txt
contains that license. MIT-licensed Kyrex source is also included; see
LICENSE-MIT.txt. Original SPDX notices remain in the corresponding source.

Messages uses mautrix/gmessages (libgm), available with its license at
https://github.com/mautrix/gmessages. The Android Go runtime comes from
golang.org/x/mobile (BSD-3-Clause).

Voice uses sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime (Apache-2.0 and MIT respectively).
Health and the native host use AndroidX (Apache-2.0) and Android Browser Helper
(Apache-2.0). Their corresponding license notices are in the published sources
and Android/Go dependency distributions used by the build.

No credentials, personal health readings, messages or Google sessions are
included in this application or its published source.
