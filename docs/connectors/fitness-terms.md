## Authorized personal use

Connect accounts and device data that you own or are authorized to access. Grant only the permissions you intend to share. The fitness integration reads authorized information and does not write health measurements or change source-provider records. It is intended for personal wellness information and activity summaries.

Connecting a source permits the integration to retrieve or upload the authorized categories as described in the [Kyrex Fitness Privacy Policy](fitness-privacy.md). Asking a Workout Bot to analyze that information can send authorized data to the model provider selected for that Bot. Review that provider and your permissions before using the service.

## Accuracy and availability

Source data may be missing, delayed, duplicated, truncated, or incomplete. Invalid timestamp intervals may be skipped. Automatic Android sync is approximate and can be delayed by connectivity, battery restrictions, or operating-system scheduling. A successful sync does not guarantee every measurement exists or was captured.

Model-generated summaries can be inaccurate. Verify important facts against the original provider data. The integration does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment and is not an emergency service. Do not use its summaries as the sole basis for medical decisions.

You may stop using the integration, disable scheduled sync, remove device permissions, or disconnect a source. Server-side disconnection and its deletion behavior are explained in the privacy policy. Disconnection does not automatically erase existing chat history or copies held by other services.

Keep your account access, OAuth credentials, server encryption keys, and phone-pairing credentials private. The server operator is responsible for its deployment configuration and the third-party services it selects.
